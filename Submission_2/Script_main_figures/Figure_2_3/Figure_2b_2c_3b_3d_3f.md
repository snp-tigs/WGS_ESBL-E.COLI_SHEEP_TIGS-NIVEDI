# Figures 2 & 3: Prevalence and MAR Index Bar Plots

**Description:**
This section covers the quantitative bar plots for phenotypic antibiotic resistance, Multiple Antibiotic Resistance (MAR) indices, and genotypic prevalence (Figures 2b, 2c, 3b, 3d, and 3f).

* **Figure 2b:** Percent prevalence of phenotypic antibiotic resistance (AST) stratified by sheep and environmental isolates.
* **Figure 2c:** Average MAR index of sheep vs. environmental isolates.
* **Figure 3b:** Percent prevalence of ESBL genes (*blaTEM, blaSHV, AmpC, blaCTX-M*).
* **Figure 3d:** Percent prevalence of tetracycline resistance genes (*tet(A), tet(B)*).
* **Figure 3f:** Percent prevalence of virulence genes (*stx1, stx2*).

### Methodology & Data Availability
These visualizations and their underlying descriptive statistics (percent prevalence, mean, standard deviation) were generated using **Microsoft Excel**. 

The raw binary presence/absence data and the calculated summary statistics used to build these charts are available in this repository. Please refer to `[Fig_2b_2c_3b_3d_3f.xlsx]` within the `data_main_figures/` directory to view the source data and charts.
